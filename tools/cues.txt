# Coaching cues for the built-in library. One exercise per line: Name|cue|cue[|cue[|cue]]  (2–4 cues: setup, execution, common fault)
# push / Chest
Barbell Bench Press|Eyes under the bar, shoulder blades pinched down and back, feet planted|Lower to the lower chest with elbows ~45–70° from the torso, then press up and slightly back over the shoulders|Don't let the butt lift or the bar bounce off the chest
Close-Grip Barbell Bench Press|Grip about shoulder width, shoulder blades set|Keep elbows tucked close to the ribs and touch lower on the chest than a normal bench|Don't go so narrow that the wrists bend back. Stack wrist over elbow
Paused Barbell Bench Press|Same setup as the bench: tight upper back, feet driving into the floor|Lower under control and hold a dead-still 1–2 s pause on the chest before pressing|Don't sink or relax during the pause. Stay tight so the bar doesn't settle into the ribs
Incline Barbell Bench Press|Bench at 30–45°, shoulder blades retracted, bar unracked over the upper chest|Lower to just below the collarbones, then press straight up over the shoulders|Don't flare elbows straight out to the sides or arch so hard it turns into a flat bench
Decline Barbell Bench Press|Hook the legs securely and set the shoulder blades before the unrack|Lower to the lower chest/sternum line and press back up over the shoulders|Use a spotter. Don't let the bar drift toward the face on the way up
Floor Press|Lie on the floor with knees bent or legs straight, bar over the chest, shoulders pinned|Lower until the upper arms touch the floor, pause briefly, then press|Don't bounce the elbows off the floor. Set them down softly
Spoto Press|Normal bench setup and grip|Stop the bar 1–2 inches above the chest and hold for a beat without touching, then press|Don't let it drift or touch. Stay tight through the pause
Pin Press|Set the safety pins at the sticking point and start each rep from a dead stop on the pins|Get tight under the bar, then drive it up explosively off the pins|Don't bounce off the pins or lose the upper-back arch between reps
Board Press|Partner holds or straps a 1–3 board stack on the chest|Lower until the bar touches the boards, pause lightly, press|Don't relax onto the boards. Keep the same tight bench setup
Smith Machine Bench Press|Line the bar up with the lower chest before unracking, shoulder blades back|Lower to the chest and press along the fixed path with elbows ~45–70°|Don't set up too far toward the head. The bar should touch mid-to-lower chest
Smith Machine Incline Press|Incline bench at 30–45°, positioned so the bar meets the upper chest|Lower under control to just below the collarbones and press up|Don't let the shoulders roll forward at the top. Keep the blades back
Dumbbell Bench Press|Kick the dumbbells up off the knees, shoulder blades back, feet planted|Lower until the dumbbells are beside the chest with forearms vertical, then press up and slightly in|Don't let the elbows drop far below the bench or the dumbbells drift apart
Incline Dumbbell Bench Press|Bench at 30–45°, dumbbells over the upper chest|Lower to the sides of the upper chest, then press up until they nearly touch|Don't arch excessively. Keep the ribs down and the shoulders back
Decline Dumbbell Bench Press|Lock the legs in on a slight decline, dumbbells over the lower chest|Lower with control to the sides of the lower chest and press up|Have a plan to get the dumbbells down safely at the end of the set
Single-Arm Dumbbell Bench Press|One dumbbell, free hand on the bench or chest, feet wide for balance|Press without letting the torso twist or roll toward the weight|Don't let the hips lift on the working side. Brace the core to stay square
Alternating Dumbbell Bench Press|Both dumbbells pressed up to lockout to start|Lower one dumbbell while the other stays locked out overhead, then switch|Don't let the locked-out arm drift or bend while the other side works
Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Bench Press|Palms facing each other, dumbbells parallel|Lower with elbows close to the sides and press straight up|Don't flare the elbows. This grip is meant to keep them tucked and shoulder-friendly
Dumbbell Floor Press|Lie on the floor, knees bent, dumbbells over the chest|Lower until the triceps touch the floor, pause, press|Don't slam the elbows into the floor
Single-Arm Dumbbell Floor Press|Lie on the floor with one dumbbell, other arm out for balance|Lower until the elbow touches the floor, then press without rolling the hips|Don't let the shoulder shrug up toward the ear
Dumbbell Squeeze Press|Press the dumbbells together hard over the chest, palms facing|Keep squeezing them together the whole time while lowering to the chest and pressing|Don't let them separate. Go lighter than a normal dumbbell press
Dumbbell Fly|Slight bend in the elbows, dumbbells over the chest, palms facing|Open the arms in a wide arc until you feel a chest stretch, then hug back up|Don't bend and straighten the elbows. That turns it into a press
Incline Dumbbell Fly|Bench at 30–45°, slight elbow bend locked in|Arc the dumbbells out and down to a stretch, then squeeze back over the upper chest|Don't go deeper than the shoulders tolerate. Stop at a strong stretch
Kettlebell Floor Press|Lie on the floor, bell resting on the back of the forearm|Press straight up and keep the wrist straight with the bell stacked over the elbow|Don't let the wrist bend back under the bell
Single-Arm Kettlebell Floor Press|Roll onto the side to get the bell into position, then lie flat|Press up, keeping the shoulder packed and the wrist neutral|Don't let the torso rotate toward the bell
Cable Fly|Pulleys at about chest height, one step forward, slight elbow bend|Bring the handles together in a hugging arc in front of the chest|Don't let the arms straighten and bend. Keep the elbow angle fixed
High-to-Low Cable Fly|Pulleys set high, staggered stance, slight forward lean|Sweep the handles down and in toward the hips|Don't shrug. Keep the shoulders down
Low-to-High Cable Fly|Pulleys set low, palms up or forward|Sweep the handles up and in to shoulder height to hit the upper chest|Don't lift with the front delts and arms. Think chest pulling the arms together
Single-Arm Cable Fly|Stand side-on to the cable, free hand on the hip|Bring one handle across in front of the chest in an arc|Don't rotate the torso to finish the rep
Standing Cable Chest Press|Split stance, handles at chest height, core braced|Press forward and slightly together, then return under control|Don't lean forward and drop into the movement. Stay upright
Single-Arm Cable Chest Press|Split stance with the opposite leg forward, brace|Press one handle forward without letting the torso twist|Don't let the cable pull the shoulder forward at the start
Machine Chest Press|Seat height so the handles line up with the mid-chest|Press out smoothly, then return slowly until you feel a chest stretch|Don't let the shoulders roll forward off the pad
Incline Machine Chest Press|Set the seat so the handles line up with the upper chest|Press up and out, controlling the return|Don't lift the hips off the seat to push heavier
Pec Deck Fly|Seat height so the arms are about shoulder level, slight elbow bend|Bring the pads or handles together and squeeze for a beat|Don't let the weight yank the arms back past a comfortable stretch
Hammer Strength Chest Press|Seat set so the handles line up with the mid-chest, back flat on the pad|Press each side evenly and control the stretch on the way back|Don't shrug or let the shoulders round forward at lockout
Push-Up|Hands just outside shoulder width, body in a straight line from head to heels|Lower the chest to the floor with elbows ~45°, then push the floor away|Don't let the hips sag or pike up
Wide Push-Up|Hands wider than shoulder width, rigid plank|Lower the chest between the hands under control|Don't go so wide that the elbows flare straight out to the sides
Diamond Push-Up|Thumbs and index fingers touching under the chest|Lower with elbows tucked back along the ribs|Don't let the hips sag. Elevate the hands if you can't keep the body straight
Incline Push-Up|Hands on a bench or box, body straight|Lower the chest to the edge, then press back|Don't drop the head or let the lower back sag
Decline Push-Up|Feet on a bench, hands on the floor under the shoulders|Lower with control and keep the hips in line|Don't pike the hips up to make it easier
Deficit Push-Up|Hands on plates or handles so the chest can go below hand level|Lower past hand height into a deep stretch, then press|Don't go deeper than the shoulders tolerate
Archer Push-Up|Hands very wide, fingers turned slightly out, body rigid|Shift down toward one hand while the other arm straightens out to the side as a helper|Don't let the hips twist or sag as you shift. Keep the body square to the floor
Weighted Push-Up|Plate on the upper back (between the shoulder blades) or a weight vest|Normal push-up: full range, rigid body|Don't let the weight shift onto the lower back. Keep it high
Band-Resisted Push-Up|Band across the upper back with ends pinned under the hands|Push explosively through the band to lockout|Don't let the band pull the hips down. Brace hard
Single-Arm Push-Up|Feet wide, working hand under the chest, free hand behind the back|Lower with the body slightly angled and press back up|Don't rotate wildly. Elevate the hands to build up to it
Ring Push-Up|Rings a few inches off the floor, body straight|Lower while keeping the rings stable, then press and turn the hands out at the top|Don't let the rings drift wide
Chest Dip|Grip the bars, lean the torso forward, knees bent behind|Lower until the upper arms are about parallel, then press up|Don't drop too deep or let the shoulders roll forward
Weighted Dip|Dip belt with the weight hanging between the legs, start locked out|Lower until the upper arms are parallel and press up under control|Don't kip or swing. Add weight only once bodyweight reps are clean
Ring Dip|Start in a support hold with the rings turned out and close to the body|Lower with the rings tight to the sides until the shoulders are just below the elbows, then press|Don't let the rings drift away from the body. Build up with support holds
Landmine Chest Press|Kneel or stand facing the landmine, bar end at chest height in both hands|Press up and forward along the bar's arc|Don't lean back to finish the press
Svend Press|Squeeze two light plates together between flat palms at the chest|Press straight out while squeezing the plates hard, then bring them back|Don't let the plates slip. The squeeze is the point, not the weight
# push / Shoulders
Barbell Overhead Press|Bar on the front delts, grip just outside the shoulders, glutes and abs braced|Press straight up, moving the head back then through so the bar finishes over mid-foot|Don't lean back and turn it into a standing incline press
Push Press|Bar in the front rack, feet hip-width|Dip straight down a few inches, then drive up hard through the legs and press to lockout|Don't let the knees travel forward or the chest drop in the dip
Behind-the-Neck Press|Only if you have the shoulder mobility. Bar on the traps, wide grip|Press straight up from behind the head and lower under control to ear level|Don't force depth or crank the neck forward
Z Press|Sit on the floor with legs straight out, no back support|Press overhead while keeping the torso tall and the ribs down|Don't lean back or round the lower back. Lighten the load if you do
Seated Barbell Shoulder Press|Bench upright, bar unracked at chin level|Press straight up, moving the head back slightly so the bar clears it|Don't arch off the backrest to finish reps
Dumbbell Shoulder Press|Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward or slightly turned in|Press up and slightly in until the arms are locked overhead|Don't arch the lower back. Squeeze the glutes and keep the ribs down
Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press|Back on an upright bench, dumbbells at shoulder height|Press up to lockout, lower to about ear level|Don't let the elbows drift behind the body
Single-Arm Dumbbell Shoulder Press|Stand tall with one dumbbell at the shoulder, free hand on the hip|Press up without leaning away from the weight|Don't side-bend. The core should stop it
Arnold Press|Start with dumbbells in front of the shoulders, palms facing you|Rotate the palms forward as you press up, then reverse on the way down|Don't rush the rotation. Keep it smooth and controlled
Kettlebell Overhead Press|Bell in the rack position, wrist straight, forearm vertical|Press up, letting the bell rotate slightly so the palm faces forward at lockout|Don't let the bell flop and bend the wrist back
Single-Arm Kettlebell Press|Clean the bell to the rack and brace|Press to lockout with the biceps by the ear|Don't lean away from the bell to finish
Bottoms-Up Kettlebell Press|Hold the bell upside down by the handle, squeeze the grip hard|Press slowly while keeping the bell balanced vertically|Don't rush it. If the bell tips, reset rather than chase it
Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Press|Half-kneel with the down knee on the same side as the dumbbell, glute squeezed|Press up and slightly forward to lockout|Don't arch the lower back or rotate toward the weight
Landmine Press|Stand staggered, bar end at the shoulder|Press up and forward along the bar's arc, reaching through at the top|Don't lean back. Let the shoulder blade move around the ribs
Single-Arm Landmine Press|Split stance with the opposite leg forward, bar end at the shoulder|Press up and out, finishing with a reach|Don't twist the torso to move the bar
Half-Kneeling Landmine Press|Half-kneel with the working-side knee down and glute squeezed|Press the bar up and forward without arching|Don't let the hips shift. Stay stacked
Landmine Push Press|Bar end at the shoulder, feet hip-width|Quick dip and drive with the legs to launch the bar, then finish with the arm|Don't press first. The legs start it
Machine Shoulder Press|Seat so the handles start at about shoulder height|Press up without locking out hard, then lower under control|Don't let the lower back peel off the pad
Smith Machine Shoulder Press|Bench under the bar so it travels just in front of the face|Press to near lockout and lower to chin level|Don't set the bench so the bar hits the nose. Check the line first
Pike Push-Up|Hips high in an inverted V, hands shoulder-width|Lower the top of the head toward the floor in front of the hands, then press|Don't let it turn into a regular push-up. Keep the hips high
Handstand Push-Up|Kick up to a wall, hands slightly wider than the shoulders|Lower the head to the floor (or a pad) under control, then press back to lockout|Don't flare the elbows or crash the head down
Wall Walk|Start in a push-up position with the feet against the wall|Walk the feet up the wall and the hands back toward it until the chest is near the wall, then reverse|Don't let the lower back sag. Keep the core tight
Dumbbell Lateral Raise|Slight elbow bend, dumbbells at the sides, slight forward lean|Raise out to the sides to shoulder height, leading with the elbows|Don't shrug or swing the weight up with momentum
Cable Lateral Raise|Cable low and on the opposite side, handle crossing in front of the body|Raise out to shoulder height with a slight elbow bend|Don't lean and yank. Keep the torso still
Machine Lateral Raise|Pads on the outside of the arms, seat so the shoulders line up with the pivot|Raise to shoulder height and lower slowly|Don't shrug the traps up to move the pads
Lean-Away Lateral Raise|Hold a post with the free hand and lean away|Raise the dumbbell out to the side for a longer range at the bottom|Don't swing. The lean adds range, not momentum
Dumbbell Front Raise|Dumbbells in front of the thighs|Raise to shoulder height with straight arms, then lower slowly|Don't lean back to swing the weight up
Plate Front Raise|Hold a plate at 3 and 9 o'clock|Raise to eye level with arms nearly straight|Don't arch the back to get it up
Cable Front Raise|Face away from a low pulley, handle between the legs|Raise forward to shoulder height|Don't let the cable pull the shoulders forward at the bottom
Rear Delt Fly|Hinge forward with a flat back, dumbbells hanging, slight elbow bend|Raise the arms out to the sides, leading with the elbows|Don't bend the elbows more as you lift. That turns it into a row
Reverse Pec Deck|Face the pad, handles at shoulder height|Sweep the arms back in an arc to about in line with the body|Don't shrug or overextend behind the body
Cable Rear Delt Fly|Cross the cables at shoulder height and grab the opposite handles|Pull the arms out and back in a wide arc|Don't turn it into a row. Arms stay nearly straight
Band Pull-Apart|Hold the band at shoulder height with straight arms|Pull the band apart until it touches the chest, then control it back|Don't shrug or arch the lower back
Face Pull|Rope at upper-chest to face height, overhand grip|Pull toward the face, separating the hands and finishing with the hands beside the ears|Don't let it become a heavy row. Light and controlled
Band Face Pull|Band anchored at face height|Pull toward the forehead with the elbows high and the hands finishing beside the ears|Don't let the elbows drop below the shoulders
Y-T-W Raise|Lie face-down on an incline bench with light dumbbells|Raise the arms into a Y, then a T, then a W, each with a short hold|Don't use momentum. The weight should be very light
Barbell Upright Row|Grip about shoulder width or wider|Pull the bar up close to the body, elbows leading, to about lower-chest height|Don't pull higher than the shoulders comfortably allow
Cable Upright Row|Straight bar on a low pulley, grip shoulder width or wider|Pull up along the body leading with the elbows|Don't go too narrow or too high if the shoulders pinch
Dumbbell External Rotation|Lie on the side, elbow pinned to the side at 90°|Rotate the forearm up, keeping the elbow in place|Don't let the elbow lift off the side
Cable External Rotation|Cable at elbow height, towel between the elbow and ribs|Rotate the hand away from the body and return slowly|Don't rotate the trunk to cheat the range
Band External Rotation|Band at elbow height, elbow tucked at 90°|Rotate out against the band, then return slowly|Don't let the elbow drift away from the body
Cable Internal Rotation|Cable at elbow height on the working side|Rotate the hand across the body with the elbow pinned|Don't use the torso to rotate
Cuban Press|Light dumbbells, upright row to elbows at shoulder height|Rotate the forearms up to vertical, then press overhead, and reverse|Very light weights only. Don't rush the rotation
Overhead Plate Raise|Hold a plate at the hips with arms straight|Raise it in front all the way overhead and lower slowly|Don't arch the lower back at the top
# push / Triceps
Close-Grip Push-Up|Hands under the shoulders or slightly narrower|Lower with the elbows tucked tight to the ribs|Don't let the elbows flare or the hips sag
Bench Dip|Hands on the edge of a bench behind you, legs out|Lower until the upper arms are about parallel, then press up|Don't drop too low or let the shoulders roll forward
Triceps Pushdown|Straight bar at a high pulley, elbows at the sides|Push down to full elbow extension and squeeze|Don't let the elbows drift forward or lean over the bar
Rope Triceps Pushdown|Rope on a high pulley, elbows pinned|Push down and spread the rope ends apart at the bottom|Don't let the shoulders roll forward to finish
Single-Arm Cable Pushdown|One handle, elbow pinned to the side|Extend fully and control the return|Don't let the elbow lift as you come up
Overhead Cable Triceps Extension|Face away from the cable, rope behind the head|Extend the arms forward and up to lockout|Don't let the elbows flare wide. Keep them in
Skull Crusher|Lie on a bench, EZ bar over the shoulders|Bend only at the elbows to lower the bar toward the forehead or just behind the head, then extend|Don't let the elbows flare out or the upper arms swing
Dumbbell Skull Crusher|Dumbbells over the shoulders, palms facing|Lower to beside the head by bending only the elbows|Don't flare the elbows. Keep the upper arms still
Overhead Dumbbell Triceps Extension|Hold one dumbbell overhead with both hands, ribs down|Lower behind the head and extend back up|Don't let the lower back arch. Brace the core
Single-Arm Overhead Dumbbell Extension|Dumbbell overhead, elbow pointing up|Lower behind the head and extend|Don't let the elbow flare out to the side
Dumbbell Triceps Kickback|Hinge forward, upper arm parallel to the floor|Extend the elbow fully and squeeze|Don't swing the weight. Keep the upper arm fixed
Cable Triceps Kickback|Hinge forward, cable from a low pulley|Extend back until the arm is straight|Don't let the shoulder drop or swing
JM Press|Close grip, bar over the shoulders|Lower toward the upper chest/chin by bending the elbows, then press|Start light. It loads the elbows hard
Tate Press|Dumbbells over the chest, elbows flared out|Lower the dumbbells to touch the chest by bending the elbows, then extend|Don't move the shoulders. It's an elbow movement
Band Triceps Pushdown|Band anchored overhead, elbows pinned at the sides|Push down to full lockout and hold a beat at the bottom|Don't let the band snap the hands back up. Control the return to about 90°
Machine Triceps Extension|Elbows aligned with the machine pivot|Extend fully and return slowly|Don't lift the elbows off the pad
Machine Dip|Sit tall with the handles beside the hips, shoulders pulled down|Press the handles down to straight arms, then return until the elbows are about 90°|Don't shrug or roll the shoulders forward at the top of the rep
# pull / Back
Pull-Up|Overhand grip just outside the shoulders, start from a dead hang|Pull the elbows down toward the ribs until the chin clears the bar|Don't kip or cut the range short at the bottom
Chin-Up|Underhand grip about shoulder width|Pull the chest toward the bar, elbows driving down|Don't flail the legs. Keep the body still
Neutral-Grip Pull-Up|Palms facing each other on parallel handles|Pull until the chin clears the handles, elbows driving down|Don't shrug at the top. Keep the shoulders down
Wide-Grip Pull-Up|Grip well outside the shoulders|Pull the chest toward the bar, thinking elbows down and out|Don't shorten the range. Go to a full hang each rep
Weighted Pull-Up|Dip belt or vest, start from a dead hang|Same strict pull-up: chin over the bar, controlled descent|Don't add weight until bodyweight reps are strict
Weighted Chin-Up|Underhand grip, weight on a dip belt|Pull to the chin over the bar with a slow lowering|Don't swing the weight to get it moving
Band-Assisted Pull-Up|Loop the band over the bar and put a foot or knee in it|Pull strictly and lower slowly, using the band only for help|Don't let the band slingshot you out of the bottom
Assisted Pull-Up Machine|Set the assistance so the last reps are hard|Pull the chin over the bar, lower under control|Don't use so much assistance that it feels easy
Negative Pull-Up|Jump or step to the top position with the chin over the bar|Lower as slowly as possible (3–5 s) to a full hang|Don't drop the last third. Control it all the way
Scapular Pull-Up|Hang from the bar with straight arms|Pull the shoulder blades down and back without bending the elbows, then relax|Don't bend the arms. It's a small shoulder-blade movement
Commando Pull-Up|Stand under the bar side-on and grip with the hands close, one in front of the other|Pull so the head goes to one side of the bar, alternating sides each rep|Don't twist the hips. Keep the body straight
Lat Pulldown|Thighs locked under the pad, grip just outside the shoulders|Pull the bar to the upper chest, driving the elbows down|Don't lean way back or pull behind the neck
Close-Grip Lat Pulldown|Close or V-handle, slight lean back|Pull to the upper chest with the elbows tight to the sides|Don't rock the torso to move the weight
Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown|Parallel-grip handle, chest up|Pull down to the upper chest and squeeze the lats|Don't let the shoulders roll forward at the bottom
Single-Arm Lat Pulldown|One handle, sit slightly side-on|Pull the elbow down to the hip|Don't rotate the torso to cheat the pull
Straight-Arm Pulldown|Bar or rope on a high pulley, slight hip hinge|Sweep the bar down to the thighs with the arms nearly straight|Don't bend the elbows. It's a lat movement, not a pushdown
Kneeling Single-Arm Pulldown|Half-kneel or tall-kneel beside the cable|Pull the elbow down to the side|Don't side-bend toward the pull
Band Lat Pulldown|Band anchored high, kneel facing it|Pull the band down to the chest, elbows to the sides|Don't arch the lower back to finish
Barbell Bent-Over Row|Hinge to about 45° with a flat back, bar hanging at the knees|Row the bar to the lower ribs, pulling the elbows back|Don't stand up with each rep or jerk the weight
Pendlay Row|Torso near parallel to the floor, bar starts on the floor each rep|Explosively row to the lower chest, then return to the floor|Don't lift the torso to help the bar up
Yates Row|Underhand grip, torso more upright (~30°)|Row the bar to the lower belly, elbows close|Don't turn it into a shrug or a hip thrust
Seal Row|Lie face-down on a high bench, bar hanging|Row to the bench while keeping the chest on the pad|Don't lift the chest off the bench to cheat
T-Bar Row|Straddle the bar, hinge with a flat back|Row the handle to the chest, squeeze the shoulder blades|Don't round the lower back or stand up into it
Meadows Row|Stand side-on to the landmine end, staggered stance, overhand grip on the bar end|Row the elbow high and back past the torso|Don't twist the torso open. The shoulder blade does the work
Landmine Row|Straddle or stand beside the bar, flat back|Row the bar to the chest/hip|Don't shrug the weight. Pull with the back
Dumbbell Row|Hinge with a flat back, dumbbells hanging|Row both dumbbells to the hips, elbows back|Don't let the lower back round
Single-Arm Dumbbell Row|Hand and knee on a bench, flat back|Row the dumbbell to the hip, elbow close to the body|Don't rotate the torso open to lift it
Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row|Chest on a 30–45° incline bench, dumbbells hanging straight down|Row the elbows back past the torso and squeeze the shoulder blades for a beat|Don't lift the chest off the pad or jerk the head up
Incline Dumbbell Row|Lie face-down on an incline bench, arms hanging, palms in|Pull the dumbbells toward the hips, keeping the elbows close|Don't shrug the weight up toward the ears. Drive the elbows back
Kroc Row|Heavy dumbbell, one hand braced, slight body English allowed|Explosive row for high reps, full stretch at the bottom|Don't let the form fall apart into a twisting yank
Renegade Row|Push-up position on dumbbells, feet wide|Row one dumbbell while keeping the hips square|Don't let the hips twist toward the rowing side
Kettlebell Row|Hinge and hold the bell, flat back|Row to the hip, elbow back|Don't let the shoulder roll forward at the bottom
Single-Arm Kettlebell Row|Staggered stance with the free hand on a bench or knee, flat back|Row the bell to the hip pocket, finishing with the elbow past the torso|Don't open the chest to the side to get the bell higher
Gorilla Row|Two kettlebells on the floor between the feet, deep hinge|Alternate rowing one bell while pushing the other into the floor|Don't rise up between reps. Stay in the hinge
Seated Cable Row|Sit tall, slight knee bend, close-grip handle|Row to the belly, squeezing the shoulder blades|Don't rock back and forth to move the weight
Single-Arm Seated Cable Row|Sit tall with one handle, the other hand on the thigh|Let the shoulder reach forward at the stretch, then row to the ribs|Don't lean the whole torso back to finish the rep
Wide-Grip Seated Cable Row|Wide bar, sit tall with a slight knee bend|Row to the lower chest with the elbows flared about 45–60° to work the upper back and rear delts|Don't rock back and forth. Keep the torso still
Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Cable Row|Half-kneel facing the cable with the working-side knee down|Row the handle to the ribs while the hips and shoulders stay square|Don't let the cable rotate the torso at the stretch
Standing Cable Row|Athletic stance facing the cable|Row the handle to the ribs while bracing to stay still|Don't lean back to counter the weight
Machine Row|Chest against the pad, seat set so the handles are at mid-chest height|Row the handles back and pause with the shoulder blades squeezed|Don't push the chest off the pad to move more weight
Chest-Supported Machine Row|Chest on the pad, seat set so the handles are at mid-chest|Row back, pause, and lower slowly|Don't lift off the pad or let the shoulders roll forward
Hammer Strength High Row|Thighs under the pad, handles overhead and in front|Pull down and back toward the chest|Don't lean back excessively
Inverted Row|Bar at about waist height, heels on the floor, body straight like a plank|Pull the lower chest to the bar and squeeze|Don't let the hips sag or lead with the chin
Ring Row|Hang under the rings with a straight body|Pull the chest to the rings and turn the palms in|Don't let the hips drop or shrug
TRX Row|Lean back holding the handles, body straight|Pull the chest up to the hands, elbows back|Don't let the hips sag or flare the elbows high
Band Row|Band anchored at chest height|Row back to the ribs and squeeze|Don't let the band snap you forward
Barbell Shrug|Bar in the hands, arms straight|Shrug the shoulders straight up toward the ears and pause|Don't roll the shoulders or bend the elbows
Dumbbell Shrug|Dumbbells at the sides, arms straight|Shrug straight up toward the ears and hold 1–2 s at the top|Don't roll the shoulders or bend the elbows to help
Trap Bar Shrug|Stand inside the trap bar, arms straight|Shrug straight up, squeeze, lower slowly|Don't bend the elbows to help
Cable Shrug|Straight bar on a low pulley, stand close, arms straight|Shrug straight up and pause at the top|Don't lean back or bounce the reps
Rack Pull|Bar on the pins or blocks at about knee height, same setup as the deadlift|Drive the hips forward to lockout, keeping the bar close|Don't lean back at the top or hitch the bar up the thighs
Back Extension|Hips on the pad, ankles locked in|Lower with a flat back and raise to a straight line|Don't hyperextend at the top
45-Degree Back Extension|Pad just below the hip crease, ankles locked in|Hinge down with a flat back, then rise to a straight line from head to heels|Don't hyperextend past straight at the top
Reverse Hyperextension|Lie face-down with the hips at the edge of the pad|Swing the legs up to about parallel using the glutes|Don't let the lower back crank into hyperextension
Superman Hold|Lie face-down with the arms extended overhead|Lift the arms, chest and legs a few inches and hold, squeezing the glutes|Don't crank the neck up. Keep the gaze on the floor
Prone Y Raise|Lie face-down, arms overhead in a Y|Lift the arms off the floor, thumbs up|Don't shrug toward the ears
Dumbbell Pullover|Lie across or along a bench, dumbbell over the chest|Lower behind the head with slightly bent arms, then pull back over|Don't bend the elbows more as you lower
Cable Pullover|Face a high pulley with a rope or bar|Sweep down in an arc to the thighs|Don't let the arms bend. Keep it lats
# pull / Biceps & Grip
Barbell Curl|Shoulder-width underhand grip, elbows at the sides|Curl up without moving the elbows forward, lower slowly|Don't swing the hips or lean back
EZ-Bar Curl|Hands on the angled part of the EZ bar|Curl to the shoulders with the elbows pinned|Don't let the elbows drift forward at the top
Dumbbell Curl|Dumbbells at the sides, palms forward|Curl both up, turning the pinky slightly up at the top|Don't swing. Control the lowering
